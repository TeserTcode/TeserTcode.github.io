2D Shapes

Triangle

Definition: A polygon with three edges and three vertices.

Etymology: From Latin "triangulum," meaning "three-cornered."

Angles: Sum of interior angles = 180°.

Regular Hexagon

Definition: A six-sided polygon with equal sides and angles.

Etymology: From Greek "hex," meaning "six," and "gonia," meaning "angle."

Angles: Each interior angle = 120°, each exterior angle = 60°.

3D Shapes

Cube

Definition: A three-dimensional shape with six square faces.

Etymology: From Greek "kubos," meaning "a cube or die."

Properties: All edges are equal, and all angles are 90°.

Surface Area: \( 6a^2 \), where \( a \) is the side length.

Volume: \( a^3 \), where \( a \) is the side length.

Sphere

Definition: A perfectly round three-dimensional shape.

Etymology: From Greek "sphaira," meaning "ball" or "globe."

Properties: All points on the surface are equidistant from the center.

Surface Area: \( 4\pi r^2 \), where \( r \) is the radius.

Volume: \( \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3 \), where \( r \) is the radius.

Theorems

Pythagorean Theorem

Statement: In a right-angled triangle, the square of the hypotenuse is equal to the sum of the squares of the other two sides.

Formula: \( a^2 + b^2 = c^2 \)

Properties of Regular Polygons

Interior Angles: The sum of interior angles of an n-sided polygon is \( (n-2) \times 180° \).

Exterior Angles: The sum of exterior angles is always 360°.
