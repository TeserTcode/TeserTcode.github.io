what does 12j mean? it appears in the quantum mechanics of angular momentum when four angular momenta are coupled. the 12j symbol is a recoupling coefficient that transforms between different coupling schemes of four angular momenta. it is used in advanced atomic and nuclear physics, particularly in the theory of complex spectra and in the description of systems with four independent angular momenta.
